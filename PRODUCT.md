# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Three overlapping audiences, each with a different arrival moment:

- **Hiring managers** at product companies and agencies — skimming dozens of
  portfolios in sequence, deciding in 30 seconds whether to read further.
  Looking for seniority, systems thinking, reliable delivery.
- **Freelance / agency clients** — evaluating José for a specific engagement
  (design system build, accessibility audit, AI workflow). Care about proven
  process and domain credibility.
- **Peers and design community** — fellow designers and developers. The quality
  of the site itself is part of the signal.

Common thread: all three want evidence that José solves the right problems well,
not that he makes things pretty.

## Product Purpose

Personal website for José Maria Barros, Principal UX & Product Designer,
Lisboa. Two jobs in one surface: showcase four case studies that demonstrate
depth of practice (WCAG auditing, UX research, AI-assisted design systems,
multi-brand architecture) and convert qualified leads for three services (Design
System as a Service, Accessibility Auditing, AI + Design Systems).

Success: a prospective client or hiring manager who arrives skeptical leaves
convinced, without José having to be in the room.

## Brand Personality

Expert, Clear, Trustworthy. The practitioner who has already solved the problem
you are worried about. No posturing, no hedging — direct competence communicated
through the quality of the work and the precision of the framing.

Voice: Portuguese, first-person, calm authority. Copy earns the reader's time
rather than demanding attention.

## Anti-references

- Flashy agency portfolios: scroll-jacking, full-screen video, heavy entrance
  animations. The medium becomes the message for the wrong reason.
- Generic Framer/Notion templates: floating blobs, hero-metric grids, identical
  card layouts, Inter-on-white with a pastel accent. Visual language of
  "I used a template."
- Behance-style image dumps: walls of screenshots with minimal framing. Work
  without the thinking behind it.
- Corporate consultant sites: stock photography, blue gradients, synergy
  language. Safe to the point of invisible.

## Design Principles

1. **The site is a proof of practice.** José builds accessible, systematic,
   WCAG-compliant products. The portfolio must demonstrate this, not just claim
   it. Every structural and visual decision is evidence.
2. **Context before screenshots.** The reader understands the problem, the
   approach, and the outcome before seeing a single mockup.
3. **Substance sets the hierarchy.** Typography, spacing, and structure carry
   the visual weight. Not color blocks, not decorative dividers, not motion for
   motion's sake.
4. **Precision over polish.** Sharp, deliberate choices beat comprehensive
   refinement. Every element has a reason; nothing decorates without earning its
   place.
5. **Trustworthiness is designed.** Clarity of language, accessibility of the
   interface, and system consistency compound into felt reliability before the
   reader consciously registers it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum; WCAG 2.2 AA target throughout. EAA 2025 compliance is a
commercial requirement for José's clients — the portfolio must itself be a
reference-quality example. Already implemented: skip link, focus-visible
management, reduced-motion media query, ARIA labeling, 44px touch targets,
semantic HTML, all text/background pairs ≥4.5:1.
